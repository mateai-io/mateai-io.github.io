How it works

Set up in three steps

No API keys, no config files to edit by hand. The app does the wiring and tells you what it changed.

  1. 1

    Install MateAI

    Download the app and drag it to Applications. Everything it needs comes bundled, so there is nothing else to install.

  2. 2

    Connect your channels

    Sign in on Google's own page and approve each permission. Read-only by default; turn on uploads and comments when you want them.

  3. 3

    Add MateAI to Claude

    One click adds a single mateai entry to Claude Desktop. Your other settings stay, and a backup is saved first.

Privacy

Your accounts stay yours

MateAI is built so you can see exactly what it touches, and undo it.

Sign-in happens on Google's page

You approve each permission yourself. MateAI never sees your password.

Stored in the macOS Keychain

Your sign-in token is kept by macOS, not in a file on disk.

Your Claude settings are kept

We add one entry, save a backup of your config first, and remove only our entry if you ask.

Nothing goes live without you

Uploads are private drafts. TikTok videos land in your inbox; you tap Post yourself.

FAQ

Questions creators ask

What do I need to use MateAI?

A Mac with Apple silicon, the Claude Desktop app and a YouTube channel. Windows and Intel Macs are not supported yet.

Is MateAI free?

Yes, MateAI is free during early access. If that changes, you'll hear about it before launch.

Will MateAI post anything without asking me?

No. Videos go up as private drafts, and TikTok videos arrive in your inbox as drafts. You publish them yourself.

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube today. TikTok drafts are waiting on TikTok's app review, and one schedule across platforms comes next.

Are there upload limits?

Google caps daily uploads for all MateAI users together until it approves more. Heavy users can bring their own Google project.

How do I disconnect?

Open MateAI and choose Disconnect on the channel, or Remove on Claude setup. Only MateAI's own entry and sign-in are removed.
