About MateAI

MateAI is made by Máté Góg, a one-person developer in Austria. “Máté” is my first name; AI is what I build with.

What I make

MateAI Studio is a Mac app for Apple silicon that connects your YouTube channel to the AI apps you already use: Claude Desktop, Codex, Cursor, Antigravity and OpenCode. You ask about your stats, prepare uploads as drafts and catch up on comments, in plain words. It isn't released yet, and it's free during early access. Join early access.

The MateAI plugin is free and works today. It's a small MCP server you install yourself on a Mac or Windows PC, and it gives Claude Code (paid Claude plan) or Google Antigravity the same kind of YouTube tools. The step-by-step setup guide needs no coding, and the source code is on GitHub.

The plugin needs a one-time setup of about 30 minutes, 15 of them for the Google developer project. That fiddly step is why MateAI Studio exists: the app does it for you, so you just sign in with Google.

The YouTube channel

I run MateAI on YouTube, a build-log channel: real things built with AI, start to finish, including the parts that didn't work.

How I work

  • No expensive stacks. I don't push a new paid tool every week. Where I can, I solve a problem inside one AI provider instead of stitching together a pile of subscriptions.
  • Measured, failures included. When I show something I built, I show the numbers and what went wrong, not just the good take.
  • No hype. No income promises, and no claims that AI will “replace” anyone. I build for real, boring, recurring problems.
  • Open skepticism. I think the way people use AI will change a lot in the next few years, and it may get less accessible and more expensive. I'd rather say that than pretend otherwise.
